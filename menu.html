Affogato

Affogato is a delightful Italian dessert that combines the richness of espresso with the creaminess of vanilla ice cream or gelato. A single shot of hot espresso is poured over a scoop of ice cream, creating a delicious contrast of temperatures and flavors. The warm espresso gradually melts the cold ice cream, resulting in a heavenly blend of bitter and sweet.
